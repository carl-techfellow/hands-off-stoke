The boards

Ranked answers with the points they are worth at single value. Round 3 doubles every number, round 4 triples it. A guess only has to land on one keyword.

green keywords also count as “busted” when Beat the Board scans a team’s output. Grey ones only help with guessing — single words like data would flag innocent output.

Fast Money

Ten questions in the deck; the game draws five per match. Player one has 20 seconds, player two has 25 and cannot repeat player one. 200 combined wins it.
